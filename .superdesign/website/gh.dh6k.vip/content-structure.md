# gh.dh6k.vip

## Business Context
- **Type/Industry:** Personal indie tech site / portfolio archive
- **What they do:** Index of experiments, tools, archives, and notes on Android and browsers
- **Target audience:** Tech enthusiasts, Android/browser community
- **Page goal:** Showcase projects and provide entry points to archives and utilities

# Page Layout & Structure

### Hero
Navy background. H1 split across two lines: "Small site." in white, "Useful signal." in magenta, both left-aligned, large display weight. Subheading in muted purple below: "A personal index for dh6k…" (2 lines). Two CTAs below: "Read in English ↗" (magenta background, dark text) and "Đọc tiếng Việt ↗" (outlined button, magenta border, transparent background). Above the heading: small red label "INDEPENDENT WEB // 2026" in all-caps, muted purple.

### Header / Navigation
Static horizontal bar. Left: "gh.dh6k.vip" logo in magenta. Right: 4 text links in muted purple (VI, EN, Filters, MOV), no primary CTA button.

### Projects and archives
- **Purpose:** Highlight key projects and archived versions
- **Layout:** H2 heading in white. Bulleted list (×3 items): magenta link text with descriptive gray text inline. Links: "t-ui dotfiles" (for Android), "YTV 16.30.34 archive", "Another YTV archive". Section label above in red (01 / SELECTED).

### Useful pages
- **Purpose:** Quick navigation to utility/reference pages
- **Layout:** H2 heading in white. Paragraph with 3 magenta links separated by center-dots: "Suggested filters", "MOV notes", "Name meaning". Section label above in red (02 / UTILITIES).

### Personal corner
- **Purpose:** Bio and language/contact options
- **Layout:** H2 heading in white. Paragraph in gray: "Student at PTIT. Interested in Android and browsers." Below: 3 magenta links separated by center-dots: "Tiếng Việt", "English", "duya-sensei@dh6k.vip". Section label above in red (03 / NOW).

### Footer
- One-line stub (3-col layout): left "Built with plain Jekyll." (muted purple), center "Sitemap" link (magenta), right "Source" link (magenta).

**Notable patterns:** Consistent section labeling in red (01/02/03). Magenta used for all interactive elements (links, CTAs) and one emphasis word in hero. Navy background throughout. Square corners. Two button styles in hero (filled magenta vs outlined). Muted purple for supporting text; gray for descriptive/secondary content.
